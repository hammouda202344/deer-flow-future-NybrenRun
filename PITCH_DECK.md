CyliaTales
Studio-grade multimodal storytelling platform — instant, scalable, and privacy-first.

Slide 1 — Title
CyliaTales

Slide 2 — Problem
Creators and brands need studio-quality narrative video, interactive books and personalized content fast, affordably, and without complex pipelines; current tools are siloed (text→video, limited customization, low-fidelity assets).

Slide 3 — Solution
A full-stack platform that turns ideas into fully rendered multimodal stories with:
- Studio-grade templates & assets
- Orchestrated pipelines (story → storyboard → render → audio)
- High-fidelity visuals, 60+ templates, 60 outfit packs
- Marketplace and enterprise tooling

Slide 4 — Traction & Opportunity
- Large creator economy (millions of indie creators + brands)
- Demand for personalized children’s content, marketing videos, and interactive narratives
- High-margin B2B licensing and marketplace revenue

Slide 5 — Business Model
- Freemium: watermark, low-res exports
- Creator Pro: unlimited HD exports
- Enterprise: private cloud, white-label, SSO
- Marketplace revenue share (templates, outfits, music)

Slide 6 — Technology Moat
- DeerFlow-based super-agent orchestration
- Hybrid renderer (WebGPU + server GPU farm)
- Asset provenance, model usage ledger, IP-safe mode
- Plugin SDK & developer APIs

Slide 7 — Go-To-Market
- Creator outreach + template partner programs
- Partnerships with music houses & education publishers
- Agency white-label pilots

Slide 8 — Team & Ask
- Founders: product, ML & rendering engineers, creative director
- Ask: $2.5M seed to build 12-month roadmap (MVP launch, marketplace seeding, 2 initial enterprise pilots)
